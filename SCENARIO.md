# SILENT CONTRACT — Scenario

*One shot. One truth.*

## Logline

In a harbour city where it has rained for eleven years, a mute ex-army marksman takes contracts from the voice in his ear, until he learns that every man he killed was about to testify against the boss he thought he was hunting, and that the voice has a family name she never told him.

## Tone

Rain-soaked noir. Short sentences, hard light, wet stone. Nobody is clean, everyone is tired, and the city keeps the receipts. Violence is quiet and final; the game never celebrates it, it counts it. Humour is dry and rare, mostly Mara's. Crane never speaks: the story is told by Mara on the phone and in his earpiece, by narration captions, and by the people around him (Inspector Brandt, Halvard) quoted in captions.

## Setting: Port Varenne

A grey harbour city on a cold northern coast. Old money built the canals and the basilica; new money built the glass towers on the Quai des Banques. Since the great flood eleven years ago the rain has never really stopped, and the **Halvard Syndicate** has grown fat on the rebuilding: the docks, the customs house, the council, the casinos and the courts.

Key places:
- **Corso Street**: bars, pensions and a neon seven. The syndicate's betting district.
- **Via Sottile / the Lavanderia**: laundries on the roofs, laundered money in the cellars.
- **Pier 9**: container yard, cranes, fog.
- **Hotel Meridian**: gold doors and short memories.
- **Canal Market**: night stalls on the quay, the Casino Luna above them.
- **Gare Lisière**: tram station, morning rush, a thousand grey coats.
- **St. Aurel Basilica**: bells silent since the war, confessions never.
- **Varenne Bridge**: the long bridge over the river mouth.
- **Hotel Calvaire**: a flophouse that rents rooms by the hour and asks nothing.
- **Quai des Banques**: the Halvard Trust bank, built like a tombstone with a doorman.
- **Cap Noir lighthouse**: the end of the breakwater, the end of the map.
- **Halvard Tower**: glass, sixty floors, a penthouse with the lights always on.

## Characters

**Elias Crane ("CRANE")** — Former army marksman, 4th Recon. Nine years ago at **Kessel Bridge** his unit was ambushed; eleven men died, Crane carried the twelfth home and has not spoken a word since. Lives alone above a closed cinema. Methodical, patient, notices everything. The player.

**Mara Voss** — Crane's handler. Feeds him contracts by phone and earpiece, pays into station lockers. Wry, precise, never sentimental, smokes when she lies. Born **Mara Halvard**: Augustin's daughter. Voss was her mother's name. Her loyalty is the twist (see below).

**Augustin Halvard, "The Banker"** — Head of the syndicate. Soft-spoken, silver-haired, white dinner jacket. Never raises his voice, never forgets a debt. Believes family is "the only account that never closes".

**Inspector Ilse Brandt** — Homicide, Port Varenne police. Honest in a city that punishes it. Smokes cheap cigarettes, draws lines between photographs on her office wall. The first to see the pattern.

**Kaspar Wendt** — Crane's spotter at Kessel Bridge, presumed dead. In truth he sold the unit's position to the enemy, was bought out of a prison camp by Halvard, and has been the syndicate's own rifle ever since. Crane's past, waiting at the end of a breakwater.

**Oskar Lund, "the Clerk"** — Halvard's lawyer. Keeps the original ledger of every bribe for twenty years, chained to his wrist in a briefcase.

**Silvio Brask** — Halvard's enforcer. Has killed eleven people, asks forgiveness for nine.

**Irène Voss** — Mara's mother, drowned in the harbour on Halvard's orders when Mara was nine. Never seen, always present.

**Inspector Jonah Reyes** — Eight years undercover inside the syndicate. Crane knows him only as "a man skimming the books".

Contract names (Act 1–2): Pietro Sanna (bookie), Tomas Rekker (launderer), Gus Malloy (dock foreman), Hendrik Szabo (customs clerk), Councilman Lionel Ashby, Dario Fenn (courier), Victor Lisle (bagman), Ruben Kahl "the Deacon" (bookkeeper).

## The twist

- **Act 1** sells the premise: Mara is an independent broker cutting the Banker's fingers off one by one.
- **Act 2** breaks it: Brandt discovers that every name on Crane's list was about to testify against Halvard. Reyes, Crane's eighth contract, was an undercover cop carrying ledger pages. The contracts were ordered by Halvard himself. Crane was the Banker's broom, and Mara was holding the handle.
- **Act 3** turns it again: Mara is Halvard's daughter. She spent twenty years becoming his most trusted instrument so she could put one rifle within one shot of him. She let his witnesses die because a trial would have given him twelve years and a way out; she wanted him in the ground. She saves Brandt, steers Crane to Wendt, takes the ledger, and stands next to her father in his penthouse while Crane takes the shot.
- **The ending** gives the last turn: with Halvard dead, Mara takes his chair. Every name Crane crossed out was her father's; every name left in the ledger is hers now. Crane, who never speaks, answers the only way he can: the full ledger lands on Brandt's desk, including the last page, written in Mara's hand.

## Story structure

### Act 1 — Chapter 1: "Rain Ledger"
*Every debt in Port Varenne is written in rain.*

Crane takes his first contracts. The targets are small and close; the rain is the main enemy. Mara is funny, efficient and seems to be on his side. Brandt notices the pattern at the end of the chapter.

### Act 2 — Chapter 2: "Glass Saints"
*The cleaner the window, the dirtier the hand.*

The targets get bigger, move, hide among look-alikes and behind guards. Clues pile up: a courier's briefcase full of receipts instead of cash, a bagman with a ticket to the prosecutor, a ledger that vanishes from a rectory within the hour. The chapter ends with Reyes on the bridge and Brandt's call at midnight.

### Act 3 — Chapter 3: "The Banker's Due"
*Every account closes.*

Mara confesses, then saves Brandt. Halvard tries to clean up his broom. Crane takes the ledger, buries his past at Cap Noir, and climbs to the last rooftop. Final confrontation with Augustin Halvard, with Mara standing beside him in the window.

## Difficulty curve

| Chapter | Distances | Wind | Targets |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (range) | 80–400 m | 1 m/s | paper dummies |
| 1 | 125–250 m | 0–2 m/s | static or slow |
| 2 | 260–370 m | 2.5–4.5 m/s | walking, identify, stealth |
| 3 | 345–515 m | 4.5–7 m/s | defend, escape, counter-sniper, finale |

Rewards rise from 300 (m1) to 1500 (m12). Rifles cost 0 / 1200 / 2600 / 4800; each gear line costs 900–2500 in total, so the player has to choose what to buy and may replay missions for stars and money.

## Missions

### Range — "Cold Range" (chapter 0, repeatable)
- **Location/time:** Varenne Rifle Club, dawn.
- **Beat:** Crane keeps his hands honest. Teodor Pell, the old range keeper, makes coffee.
- **Situation:** Ten paper targets on stands at 80, 120 (×2), 160, 200 (×2), 250, 300, 350 and 400 m, spread across an earth range with distance boards. Wind 1 m/s. Teodor (ally) stands by his hut on the right.
- **Objectives:** Knock down all ten targets. Do not shoot Teodor.
- **Reward:** 120, repeatable.

### Chapter 1 — Rain Ledger

#### m1 — "Wet Paint" (tutorial)
- **Location/time:** Corso Street, 23:40, night.
- **Beat:** First name on the list. Mara introduces herself and the job.
- **Situation:** Crane on a rooftop across the street. Pietro Sanna (grey coat, red tie) smokes outside the Lucky 7 bar at 125 m, standing still. A woman with an umbrella and a man walk the street; a drunk leans on a lamp; a bartender and a tenant are in lit windows. No wind.
- **Tutorial:** Mara's earpiece lines teach drag to aim, scope, zoom, hold breath, fire and bullet drop.
- **Objectives:** Eliminate Pietro Sanna. Do not harm civilians.
- **Plot:** "One finger off the Banker's hand."

#### m2 — "Laundry Day"
- **Location/time:** The Lavanderia roofs, Via Sottile, 05:50, rain.
- **Beat:** Tomas Rekker takes calls on the roof because he thinks rain cannot be wiretapped.
- **Situation:** Rekker (tan coat, umbrella, blue tie) paces slowly on the Lavanderia roof at 190 m. His minder stands at the chimney on the same roof, his driver smokes on the next roof at 200 m. A woman hangs laundry on another roof; pedestrians with umbrellas below. Wind 1.2 m/s from the left. If a guard raises the alarm, Rekker runs for the stairwell door.
- **Objectives:** Eliminate Tomas Rekker before he reaches the stairwell. Do not harm civilians.
- **Plot:** Halvard will hear by breakfast.

#### m3 — "Dockside Arithmetic"
- **Location/time:** Pier 9, 05:30, fog.
- **Beat:** Two names, one meeting: the dock foreman and the customs clerk who stamps his crates.
- **Situation:** Across the harbour water, on the container dock at 235 m. Malloy walks slowly between container stacks, Szabo waits by his briefcase near the Pier 9 warehouse. A driver and a dock patrol guard them; a dock worker potters on the left. Fishing boats in the foreground. Wind 1.5. Once the first shot rings out the guards start their alarm count and the survivor runs for the warehouse door.
- **Objectives:** Eliminate Gus Malloy and Hendrik Szabo.
- **Plot:** Mara: "You're good, Crane. That worries me a little."

#### m4 — "The Meridian"
- **Location/time:** Hotel Meridian, Boulevard Ostre, 19:20, dusk.
- **Beat:** Councilman Lionel Ashby, who votes the way Halvard pays.
- **Situation:** Ashby (grey hair, gold tie) stands in his lit suite window on the hotel face at 250 m; his escort is in the next window, a guest further down. A bodyguard on the balcony below. On the boulevard at 160 m: the limousine, a driver, a doorman, passers-by. Wind 2. If the alarm sounds Ashby retreats into the suite.
- **Objectives:** Eliminate Councilman Ashby. Do not harm civilians.
- **Plot:** Inspector Brandt is introduced. "Somebody is pruning the Halvard Syndicate. Or somebody is pruning it for him."

### Chapter 2 — Glass Saints

#### m5 — "Canal Market"
- **Location/time:** Canal Market under the Casino Luna, 22:15, night.
- **Beat:** Brandt leaves her card under Crane's door. Mara tells him to ignore it. First moving target.
- **Situation:** Dario Fenn (camel coat, fedora, briefcase) walks the quay at 260 m from stall to stall, a bodyguard one step behind him. Vendors, strollers, a lit casino window, the canal and its boats in front. Wind 2.5 gusting. Lead the target. If the alarm sounds he runs for the left alley.
- **Objectives:** Eliminate Dario Fenn. Do not harm civilians.
- **Plot:** Fenn's briefcase is full of receipts, not cash. First crack in the story.

#### m6 — "Same Coat, Different Man" (identify the target)
- **Location/time:** Gare Lisière tram platform, 08:05, rain.
- **Beat:** Victor Lisle knows someone wants him dead and has hired men to wear his coat.
- **Situation:** No target markers. On the platform at 320 m walk Lisle and four look-alikes in grey coats; each decoy matches all but one detail (blue tie, umbrella instead of briefcase, a cap instead of a fedora, no hat). Only Lisle has fedora + red tie + briefcase. A watcher guards the platform end. Commuters, a newspaper seller. Wind 3 with rain from the left. Killing any civilian fails the contract.
- **Objectives:** Identify and eliminate Victor Lisle. Do not harm civilians.
- **Plot:** Lisle carried a train ticket to the capital and an appointment with the prosecutor. Brandt draws lines on her wall; all of them lead to HALVARD.

#### m7 — "Choir of Wolves" (stealth, no alarm)
- **Location/time:** St. Aurel Basilica, 04:40, fog.
- **Beat:** The enforcer and the bookkeeper in one churchyard.
- **Situation:** Silvio Brask (black leather, cigarette) paces the churchyard at 340 m with a priest. Ruben Kahl, the Deacon, works in the lit rectory window at 360 m. Guards: a patrol walking the churchyard past Brask, a lookout on the nave roof, a guard at the rectory door, a sentry on the cemetery wall far left. A nun tends the graves. Wind 4. Any alarm fails the mission. A suppressor turns this into a puzzle; without one, the first shot starts every guard's count and the player has seconds to finish. The patrol finds Brask's body if it lies on his route.
- **Objectives:** Eliminate Silvio Brask and Ruben Kahl. Do not raise the alarm. Do not harm civilians.
- **Plot:** The Deacon's ledger vanishes from his desk within the hour.

#### m8 — "The Messenger"
- **Location/time:** Varenne Bridge, 06:20, dawn.
- **Beat:** Mara meets Crane in person for the first time, in a car. Her hands shake when she lights the cigarette.
- **Situation:** Jonah Reyes (olive coat, briefcase) walks the long bridge deck at 370 m, past a stalled dawn jam of cars, stopping at the middle to wait for a buyer. Two "tails" follow him and a man waits at the midpoint. A jogger, a fisherman, an umbrella. River and barges below, dog-walkers on the embankment at 160 m. Wind 4.5 gusting. If the alarm sounds Reyes runs for the end of the bridge.
- **Objectives:** Eliminate Jonah Reyes. Do not harm civilians.
- **Plot (end of Act 2):** Reyes was an undercover inspector bringing ledger pages to Brandt. Brandt calls Crane: every man on his list was going to testify against Halvard. "You are not hunting the Banker, Crane. You are his broom."

### Chapter 3 — The Banker's Due

#### m9 — "Cut Loose" (defend)
- **Location/time:** Hotel Calvaire, Rue des Pendus, 01:10, rain.
- **Beat:** Mara admits Halvard paid for every bullet. Then she warns him: Halvard's crew is on the way to kill Brandt.
- **Situation:** Brandt (ally) waits in a lit window of the Hotel Calvaire at 350 m. Ten attackers come in waves along the street at 345 m from both ends and out of the alleys, and one over the rooftops, all heading for the hotel door. Pedestrians and a tenant in a window. Wind 4.5 gusting 2. If one reaches the door, Brandt dies. Shooting Brandt fails the mission.
- **Waves:** t0 two from the far ends; t18 two runners from the edges; t40 two from the alleys, close; t65 one on the rooftops + one runner; t90 the last two.
- **Objectives:** Stop every attacker before he reaches the hotel door. Keep Inspector Brandt alive.
- **Plot:** Brandt: "Your handler called me an hour before they came." Mara reveals she is Mara Halvard. Her father drowned her mother. "I needed you to become his most useful broom. Now we sweep him."

#### m10 — "Paper Boats" (timed escape)
- **Location/time:** Quai des Banques, 18:50, dusk.
- **Beat:** With his witnesses dead, only the original ledger can still hang Halvard "and everyone else with him".
- **Situation:** Oskar Lund (charcoal coat, burgundy tie, briefcase) waits on the steps of the Halvard Trust at 420 m, then walks to his armoured car. A minder ahead, a minder behind, a driver by the car, a rifleman on the bank roof. Dockers and a bank clerk. Harbour basin and boats in front. Wind 5.5 gusting. 80-second timer; if Lund reaches the car he escapes with the ledger.
- **Objectives:** Eliminate Oskar Lund before he reaches the armoured car. Do not harm civilians.
- **Plot:** The briefcase disappears from the quay before the police arrive. "It's somewhere safe. With me."

#### m11 — "Old Friends" (counter-sniper, timed)
- **Location/time:** Cap Noir lighthouse, 05:10, fog.
- **Beat:** Kessel Bridge, nine years ago. Kaspar Wendt is alive and hunting Crane.
- **Situation:** Wendt (rifle) walks the lighthouse gallery at 503 m sweeping the coast; his spotter Lukas Merle stands on the breakwater at 500 m. Two guards patrol the breakwater; the keeper is in his cottage window; a fisherman on the rocks. Patrol boat in the bay. Wind 6.5 gusting. 100-second timer before Wendt spots Crane's glint.
- **Objectives:** Eliminate Kaspar Wendt and his spotter before Wendt finds you. Do not harm civilians.
- **Plot:** The last of Kessel Bridge goes into the sea. Halvard has nobody left but himself, and his daughter.

#### m12 — "The Banker's Due" (finale, no alarm)
- **Location/time:** Halvard Tower, 02:00, rain.
- **Beat:** Halvard believes Mara has brought him the broom's address. She has: his cars are on their way to an empty flat.
- **Situation:** Augustin Halvard (white dinner jacket, silver hair, armoured vest, hp 160) walks back and forth in his penthouse window at 515 m. Mara (ally, red coat) stands in the same window line; Halvard walks past her. Three bodyguards on the terrace, two riflemen on neighbouring roofs. A night clerk and a cleaner in lower windows. Wind 7 gusting. Any alarm and Halvard is in his elevator. A body shot from a lesser rifle will not stop him: head, or a heavy rifle.
- **Objectives:** Eliminate Augustin Halvard. Do not hit Mara. Do not raise the alarm.
- **Plot:** "Thank you, Elias. Go home. Don't call. You never do."

## Ending

Halvard dies between one word and the next. Mara does not leave the penthouse; she sits in his chair. Every name Crane crossed out was her father's; every name left in the ledger is hers now. At dawn a parcel reaches Inspector Brandt's desk: the complete Halvard ledger, with a last page written in a woman's hand, beginning with M. At noon Mara's phone rings. Nobody speaks. Nobody ever did. She knows exactly who it is.

*One shot. One truth.*
